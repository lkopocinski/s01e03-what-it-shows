How to tell them apart

  • parser: the booked date and the date we sent are the same two numbers, day and month swapped (7 April → 4 July). It happened to every tenant that writes the day first, through any channel. Fixed.
  • hint: the same swap, but only through the booking form of tenants on booking-v3. That form printed MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, so people typed the month first, and the form then read it day first. The parser fix never reached it. The form is retired. #4471’s own table already showed it as no ticket of its own.
  • one-day: the date lands one day off, not swapped: Tuesday became Monday, a meeting fell on different days in Lisbon and Toronto, only June, an invite older than its booking. No day/month swap gives a one-day move, so neither fix touches it. It points to a timezone conversion. The desk read these the same way (desk-notes.json) and files them under parser by tenant, so they are counted there too.
