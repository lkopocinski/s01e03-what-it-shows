How to tell them apart

  • parser: the booked date and the date we sent are the same two numbers, day and month swapped (7 April → 4 July). It happens to every tenant that writes the day first, through any channel. The queued parser fix is for this one.
  • hint: the same swap, but only through the booking form of tenants on booking-v3. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, so people type the month first, and the form then reads it day first. The parser fix does not reach it; the form’s hint has to change. #4471’s own table already shows it as no ticket of its own.
  • one-day: the date lands one day off, not swapped: Tuesday became Monday, a meeting fell on different days in Lisbon and Toronto, only June, an invite older than its booking. No day/month swap gives a one-day move. That points to a timezone conversion. The desk already read these the same way (desk-notes.json), but causes.ts files them under parser by tenant, so they had no ticket.

Why one ticket hid them

  • Decision 0001 merges on subject alone. “Wrong day” reads the same whatever the cause.
  • Intake tags come from the reporter’s browser, not the tenant, and disagree with the cause on dozens of these reports. Count by tenant and channel, then read the bodies.
  • The export repeats some reports. Count unique ids.

What changes on the desk
